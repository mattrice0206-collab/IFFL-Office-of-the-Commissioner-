Commissioner Memorandum No. 26-001

Establishment of the Office of the Commissioner

Issued: 2026

Status: Active

Purpose

This Memorandum formally establishes the Office of the Commissioner as the executive authority responsible for the administration of the Israeli Fantasy Football League.

Authority

The Commissioner shall possess all powers granted under the League Constitution and Bylaws, including the authority to administer League affairs, interpret governing documents, certify League records, review transactions, issue disciplinary actions, and publish official League directives.

Implementation

This Memorandum shall become effective immediately upon publication and shall remain in force unless amended or rescinded.

Certification

Issued under the authority of the Commissioner.
