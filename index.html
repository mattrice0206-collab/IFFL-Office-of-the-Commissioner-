Official League Office

The Office of the Commissioner serves as the official administrative authority of the Israeli Fantasy Football League. All league rulings, memorandums, trade decisions, governing documents, and official records published through this office constitute the official record of the League.

Mission Statement

The Office of the Commissioner is committed to ensuring the fair, orderly, and professional administration of the Israeli Fantasy Football League. Through impartial governance, transparent decision-making, and comprehensive record keeping, the Office seeks to preserve competitive integrity while providing every league member with a structured and enjoyable fantasy football experience.
