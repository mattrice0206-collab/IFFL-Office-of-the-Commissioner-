Israeli Fantasy Football League

Office Notice No. 26-001

Welcome to the Inaugural Season

Date Issued: 2026

The Office of the Commissioner officially welcomes all League Members to the inaugural season of the Israeli Fantasy Football League.

The establishment of this League marks the beginning of a long-term commitment to competitive integrity, sportsmanship, transparency, and responsible League governance.

League Members are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the Constitution, League Bylaws, Draft Rules, and all publications issued by the Office of the Commissioner.

Future Office Notices will communicate League announcements, scheduling information, meeting dates, and administrative updates throughout the season.


Issued by the authority of the Commissioner.
