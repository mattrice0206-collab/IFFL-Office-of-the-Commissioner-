Mission Statement

The Office of the Commissioner exists to provide impartial leadership, preserve competitive balance, enforce League rules, and maintain complete transparency in League governance. Every official action undertaken by the Office is intended to protect the long-term integrity of the Israeli Fantasy Football League.

Authority of the Office

  • Interpret and enforce the Constitution and Bylaws.
  • Approve or deny League transactions.
  • Issue official League Memorandums.
  • Investigate potential rules violations.
  • Administer League discipline.
  • Maintain official League records.
  • Preside over League meetings.

League Governance

League governance is conducted pursuant to the Constitution and League Bylaws. The Commissioner serves as the League's chief executive officer while the Deputy Commissioner provides administrative assistance and assumes delegated responsibilities as assigned.

Office History

The Office of the Commissioner was established in 2026 upon the founding of the Israeli Fantasy Football League. Since its establishment, the Office has served as the League's permanent administrative authority responsible for preserving League history, maintaining official records, and ensuring the orderly administration of League affairs.
